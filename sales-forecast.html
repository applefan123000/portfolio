How the forecast works

Holt-Winters tracks level, trend and a 12-month seasonal pattern with exponential smoothing (parameters fitted by grid search). Trend + seasonal indices fits a straight-line trend by least squares and applies each calendar month’s average seasonal index. Seasonal naïve repeats last year’s same month, scaled by the recent year-over-year trend. Intervals widen with horizon (∝ √h) from each method’s residual spread, using z = 1.28 (80%) or 1.96 (95%). Figures are planning estimates, not guarantees — re-run monthly as new actuals land.

Built for sales & operations planning: demand reviews, quota setting, and inventory conversations. Sample data is illustrative monthly revenue for a mid-size retail business (Jan 2023 – Sep 2026), with a Nov–Dec seasonal peak.

    Analysis methods

    Every metric and narrative insight is calculated from the same dataset in the page, keeping charts and commentary aligned.

    • Quarter-over-quarter: Q3 vs. Q2
    • Revenue bridge: isolates volume and value/mix effects
    • Contribution: segment change / net portfolio change
    • Health signal: four equally weighted evidence checks

    Data readiness & analytical coverage

    What the current file can support—and where interpretation needs caution

    Interpretation boundary

    These are descriptive diagnostics, not causal proof. A segment correlation or period-over-period movement should be validated against campaign, inventory, pricing, and operational context before action.

    Comparison logic

    The latest three monthly periods are compared with the preceding three when six or more months are available. Shorter files are split into earlier and later halves.

    How it works

    Model & definitions

    • Demand: promo units = baseline units × (1 − discount)^elasticity (constant-elasticity demand, the standard first-pass promo model).
    • Incremental profit = promo profit − baseline profit over the same weeks, after vendor funding and fixed promo costs.
    • Promo investment = markdown given away on promo units (discount × price × promo units) + fixed cost − vendor funding. ROI = incremental profit ÷ promo investment.
    • Break-even lift = unit increase needed for incremental profit to hit exactly $0 at that discount.
    • Vendor funding is modeled as a % of the total markdown cost the supplier reimburses.

    What it doesn't model

    • Halo / basket effects (promo shoppers buying other full-margin items) and cannibalization of sister products.
    • Post-promo dip from stockpiling (pull-forward), competitor response, and stock-outs.
    • Treat the output as a planning estimate: sanity-check the elasticity against your last comparable promotion.

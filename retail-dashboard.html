Sales / sq ft = period sales ÷ store size. It normalizes performance for store size, so different-sized stores can be compared fairly — a small store can trail on total sales yet lead per square foot.

Method: Sales = Traffic × Conversion × Average basket. For the demo, estimated driver effects are scaled to match total variance; for uploads, missing effects are grouped as a residual. These are diagnostic estimates, not causal proof. All figures are a fictional demo dataset for portfolio purposes.

Design the two plans

How payouts are calculated

Attainment = a rep's sales ÷ quota. The four rate bands key off attainment: below 80%, 80–100%, 100–120% (the accelerator zone), and above 120%.

Marginal (bracket) mode — each band's rate applies only to the sales inside that band, like income-tax brackets. A rep at 130% attainment earns the top rate only on the slice above 120%.

Retroactive mode — the rate of the highest band reached applies to all of the rep's sales. Simpler to explain, but a rep sitting at 99% attainment has a much bigger cliff to jump than in marginal mode.

Kicker — a flat bonus paid once attainment reaches the kicker threshold. Floor — a guaranteed minimum payout (a draw/guarantee). Cap — the maximum any rep can earn; applied last, after the floor.

SPIFF / bonus — a one-time amount set per rep in the roster. It is added to the payout after the floor and cap are applied, so even a capped rep receives it in full. The same SPIFF applies under both plans, so it raises both totals equally and never changes the B − A gap.
